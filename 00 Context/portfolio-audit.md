Date: November 2, 2026

## Scope and Evidence

This is a read-only review of the Framer source project, not a verified review of the published site. No Framer content or configuration was changed. Read-only program responses reported zero changed nodes.

Reviewed the page inventory, root metadata, available text from the six dedicated project pages, the shared project-detail page, and desktop screenshots of Home, About, and Damyller plus a mobile Home screenshot. Some long text results were truncated by Framer, so project recommendations concern the inspected excerpts and section structure rather than a complete sentence-by-sentence edit. Component and CMS content was not exhaustively extracted. Screenshots expose more content than the direct text extraction because some text is rendered through components.

The requested `portfolio-audit` skill was not found in the project or the local Codex skill/plugin folders. This document is a direct audit against the approved brief. `visual-direction.md` does not yet exist. Existing visual assets are candidates for reuse, not approved final visual specifications.

No live navigation, form submission, animation playback, keyboard behavior, reduced-motion behavior, performance, or formal contrast measurements were tested. Do not interpret these as passing or failing. No evidence here justifies deleting any project.

## Overall Assessment

The existing identity already expresses personality through distinctive lettering, personal photography, a monogram, and a green accent. Preserve that creative intent for consideration in visual direction. The larger mismatch is editorial: introductory and career copy emphasizes product design and leadership more heavily than the approved senior hands-on, multidisciplinary positioning. The home page also behaves partly like a full resume, delaying work and contact access.

## Keep

- The multidisciplinary project range: digital interfaces, games, brand systems, editorial work, and packaging support the approved positioning.
- The image-based index, individual project pages, and project cover imagery. These match approved structural decisions.
- The personal photography and expressive identity as visual-direction inputs. Their exact treatment and the green color remain to be approved.
- Project summaries with Role, Date, and Scope, after checking accuracy. They make contributions easier to scan.
- Damyller's editorial publications and print/retail applications. They demonstrate experience beyond interface design.
- Related-project navigation and a clear route back to the work index, subject to link verification.
- Berlin location, direct contact information, and professional social access. Keep contact easy to find.

## Improve

- Shorten the path from introduction to selected work. The mobile screenshot makes the long introduction and resume sections particularly apparent.
- Balance the home selection across disciplines. The four visible cards are Real Quiz Hero, Quiz Bingo, TerraYou, and Damyller. Consider a brand or packaging project if its assets and contribution are strong enough; do not add it automatically.
- Use project-specific section menus. Brand, editorial, and game work should not inherit identical UX storytelling requirements.
- Explain what I personally designed, directed, or coordinated, and credit other contributors. Photography-heavy galleries especially need clear authorship boundaries.
- Select fewer, more purposeful images for long galleries. Pair visual evidence with the decision or deliverable it demonstrates.
- Review small green text and thin green borders against the light background. Screenshots suggest a readability concern, but contrast ratios have not been measured.
- Distinguish structured content from decorative spacing. The captured Home has a large interval before Education, and Damyller has another between gallery sections. Check whether these are intentional, motion-related, or layout issues before changing them.
- Treat scroll-driven movement and cursor responses as future explorations. A static screenshot cannot establish the current animation behavior. The Damyller accordion effect remains exploratory, as specified in the brief.

## Rewrite

- Home hero and About: reduce repeated phrases such as intuitive experiences, scalable systems, and business goals/user needs. Replace broad claims with actual areas of contribution and a friendly first-person voice.
- Experience: replace duplicate role descriptions with specific responsibilities and progression. Preserve historically accurate titles rather than retroactively renaming jobs to match current positioning.
- Project introductions: state context, personal role, collaborators, constraints, and deliverables plainly. Separate intentions from demonstrated results.
- Claims such as TerraYou flows being “validated,” Quiz Bingo reducing cognitive overload, and design changes improving collaboration need supporting evidence or wording that describes design intent. This audit does not establish that those claims are false.
- Repeated “Learnings & Growth” endings should earn their place with project-specific reflection. Remove them where they repeat generic statements rather than useful evidence.
- Course descriptions should remain clearly educational. Verify the formal credential wording for the Udemy entry “Master Digital Product Design” so it cannot be mistaken for an academic master's degree.
