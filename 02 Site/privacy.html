Hosting: GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit a page, your browser connects to GitHub’s servers, which receive technical data such as your IP address, the requested page, the date and time of the request, and your browser’s user agent. According to GitHub, the IP address of every visitor to a GitHub Pages site is logged and stored for security purposes. I have no access to these logs.

Purpose and legal basis: providing this website reliably and securely. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, my legitimate interest in operating a functional and secure website.

Retention: GitHub does not publish a specific retention period for these logs. It states that it keeps personal data for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected and to meet legal obligations. More information: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

I use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a service provided by Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, to understand how this portfolio is used, for example which pages are visited and how quickly they load.

How it works: when analytics is active, your browser loads a script from static.cloudflareinsights.com. The script reads the address of the page you are viewing, the referring page, your browser’s user agent, and page-load performance data, and sends this information to Cloudflare. As with any connection, Cloudflare also receives your IP address. The script does not set cookies and does not use your browser’s storage. According to Cloudflare, query strings in page addresses are not recorded.

Opting out: analytics is active by default. On your first visit, a notice informs you about it and lets you decline. You can change your choice at any time in , which is also available at the bottom of every page. If you decline, the script is no longer loaded on subsequent pages or visits in this browser, and any further data the script would send from the current page is discarded. Data transmitted before you declined cannot be withdrawn retroactively.

Purpose and legal basis: understanding how the portfolio is used in order to improve it. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, my legitimate interest in understanding how the website is used. You can object to this processing at any time by declining in Privacy settings (Art. 21 GDPR).

Retention: according to Cloudflare, analytics data is kept unsampled for 7 days and is then aggregated to about 10% of its original volume for long-term storage. The statistics are available to me for the previous six months. More information: Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Your privacy preference

When you select “Decline” or “Got it”, or change your choice in Privacy settings, your choice is stored in your browser’s local storage under the key pos-analytics. It remains on your device and is not transmitted to me or to third parties. Storing it is necessary to remember the choice you made. You can delete it at any time by clearing this website’s data in your browser. If your browser blocks local storage, your choice applies only to the current page.
