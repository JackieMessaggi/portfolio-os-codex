About Easter lily

Easter lily (Lilium longiflorum) is a bulbous perennial known for its large, white, trumpet-shaped flowers. It is commonly grown as an ornamental plant, especially around Easter.

Scientific name Lilium longiflorum
Common name Easter lily

Plant care

For growing the plant. Not about pet safety.

Light Bright light, protected from intense direct sun.
Water Keep the growing medium moderately moist, avoiding waterlogging.
Soil Fertile, organic-rich, well-drained soil.
Cultivation reference: Royal Horticultural Society (RHS) Candidate content. Sources not yet verified.

Pet Poison Helpline Supports: classification; all plant parts; eating any part of a lily from the Lilium genus; water from a vase holding lilies. Refers to: lilies (Lilium genus) · cats · Independence: counts as one source together with MSD Veterinary Manual.
Cats Protection Supports: classification; pollen picked up by rubbing against the flower and licking the fur; advice not to have lilies in the home or garden. Refers to: lilies · cats · Independence: not verified.
