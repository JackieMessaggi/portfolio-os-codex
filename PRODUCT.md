## Users

## Product Purpose

Jackie Messaggi's portfolio presents her multidisciplinary experience and the quality of her work to attract senior hands-on design opportunities. Success means communicating her actual strengths clearly and supporting conversations about roles aligned with them. No conversion targets or measured results have been established.

## Positioning

Senior Designer | Brand, Product & Creative Systems. Jackie is based in Berlin and has 18+ years of experience spanning graphic design, brand identity, art direction, editorial work, UI, digital products, design systems, and hands-on design leadership.

The portfolio should attract senior UI, visual, brand, and visual-system roles, including English-speaking opportunities in Berlin or remotely. It should not position her as an end-to-end Senior Product Designer, a research or analytics specialist, or a people manager seeking primarily management work. Product Design, AI-assisted workflows, and creative coding include areas of current exploration; distinguish educational exploration from demonstrated professional experience.

## Product Principles

1. Present actual experience and individual contribution with clear evidence and attribution.
2. Show multidisciplinary range with a coherent professional focus.
3. Make project work easy to reach and assess; expressive interactions must support it.
4. Match storytelling to the discipline rather than forcing every project into a UX case-study template.
5. Preserve the owner's approved decisions and distinguish proposals, educational work, prototypes, and verified outcomes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support desktop and mobile, keyboard and touch access, readable content, and reduced motion. Provide a normal vertical reading alternative to future rotation and horizontal card movement. Do not require hover or animation to understand essential content or reach work. No formal accessibility conformance has been verified or claimed.
